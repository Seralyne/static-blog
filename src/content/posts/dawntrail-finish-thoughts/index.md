# Side Content 

Moving onto the side content, this is a bit difficult to do in a linear fashion, so I figure I might as well start with the raid series.

## And his name is John Final Fantasy!
The Arcadion - or Nvidia (Savage) in reference to the shape of the building itself, is... certainly a thing. And whether you like the story as a whole really depends on how much you like wrestling tropes, and how immersed into wrestling you are.

I, for one, am not into wrestling, have never been, and probably never will be, so story-wise, the last twist of the raid series genuinely kind of ruined the story for me. I get that it's a wrestling trope, but it's one that doesn't resonate with me, for a similar reason that the Endwalker post-story didn't resonate with me: I get what it's a reference to, *Final Fantasy IV*, but the reference is lost on me, so it lands kind of hollow.

The fights themselves, and the visual and audio presentation? Actual masterpieces basically every single one. I'm speaking from primarily a normal mode perspective for these - I've done a liiittle bit of Dancing Green on Savage and a liiittle bit of Vamp Fatale on Savage as well. I do suppose I should start by addressing the Tom Morello-shaped elephant in the room.

Good lord, the track Tom Morello and Beartooth composed for the game absolutely fucks. And so does the fight. I've got my gripes about how we got there, but the fight itself in all its presentation absolutely deserves the spot as the final raid of the series.

Another stand-out, at least to me, is Chrissy Costanza's performance in Give It All. I had never imagined that the artist behind *Legends Never Die* from League of Legends would perform in one of my other favourite games, but it was definitely satisfying when I connected the dots.

In terms of other great musical performances, obviously Bee My Honey is great, though I was pleasantly surprised by both Sinister and Back to the Drawing Board. Both of those grew on me with time. 

Fight-wise, I quite enjoyed what I've seen of Dancing Green on Savage, and the Brute Bomber fights were also great fight-wise. The Tyrant was also great on normal. 

In general, I appreciate what's changed from Endwalker too, though. Gone are the days of the supermassive targetboxes, which frankly got ridiculous for bosses like Kokytos in Pandæmonium, but I also appreciate the experimentation with fight arenas as well - they're mostly square or spherical in Arcadion, but a lot of them have at least some level of arena gimmick, so I can forgive that. It is also arguably on-theme for the raid series, given it's supposed to be a series of wrestling arenas.


## Do Vana'diel Reconstructions Dream of Electrope Sheep?
I'm not a FF11 player. Like, not at all. But I will admit I've been curious about Vana'diel ever since I got to play the crossover with Iroha back during the downtime before Endwalker. It has been quite a while since I've done the fights, but I have quite enjoyed them - and the story too. It was good to see Alxaal and Prishe and Shantotto properly. It was great that Bakool Ja Ja got more story too! Quite enjoyed his character by the end of Dawntrail's MSQ, and it's been good seeing him trend further in that direction too.

Was interesting to see other takes on Omega, Alexander, Shinryu and Ultima as well. 

## Other Side Content


### Dungeons

The dungeons were great. The difficulty had gone from the absolute snoozefest that was the Endwalker dungeons to well... "Not difficult but you might wipe once or twice if everyone's blind" which, in my opinion, is the ideal difficulty for required story content. I wouldn't mind them a little bit more difficult, but on the other hand I also saw people on the forums talking about how Endwalker's dungeons were too hard (not even Dawntrail's!) and well... maaaybe the current middle ground is acceptable!


### High-End Content

I'm not an ultimate raider myself, my progress extends to having seen the very beginning of Ifrit in The Weapon's Refrain (UWU), nor do I find myself particularly drawn to the Variant Dungeons, I do love myself a good spectacle, and I'm an avid follower of the Race to World First whenever a new series of difficult content comes out, and while Futures Rewritten was a shorter race than previous ones, like Dragonsong's Reprise or The Omega Protocol, it was an enjoyable spectacle nonetheless. It was not without its dramas, of course, but at this point, none of them are.

Dancing Mad, however, came in as a bit of a surprise. I was a bit disappointed at the announcement myself - I have not played *Final Fantasy VI*, and did not catch up with the story in *Final Fantasy XIV* until shortly before Patch 5.2 launched so my only exposure to Kefka prior to Dancing Mad (or UMAD, for short) was what little we saw of him in the Omega raid series on normal mode. I've been having the idea of a "Ascians Ultimate" or "Zenos Ultimate" for a while now. I do believe those ideas are probably also cooking over at Creative Studio 3 at Square Enix, but those will have to wait and see in Evercold itself.


### Cosmic Exploration

I did not do much of Cosmic Exploration, but what little I did do was quite enjoyable - it was a lot closer to the Ishgard Restoration than I expected it to be, and that was genuinely enjoyable. I do miss having a seasonal leaderboard to grind for rather than just the bi-weekly Star Crafter thing, but that is also pretty much my only complaint. The planets we explored were nice, but framing-wise I probably would have favored a more traditional rebuilding effort. Thematically, Cosmic Exploration feels more it should be adventurer-ey (like, say, a Field Exploration) than a crafter/gatherer thing. 

### Occult Crescent

Speaking of Field Operations! I've really been sleeping on the Occult Crescent - but from what I've played, this is pretty close to what I'd want out of one of them. I'm a lore gal, and you're telling me I can get lore from *both* Skalla, Amdapor and Nym in one place? Sign me up! I've yet to even look into the North Horn at all. Perhaps there's a weekend project for me while watching BlizzCon, which is the weekend of the week of writing this blogpost.
